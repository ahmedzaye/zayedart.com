Za Snake.

The Classic Snake Game, Reimagined.

Slither. Collect. Customize. Beat your best.

Za Snake is a modern take on the classic Snake game, designed for Android and developed by Ahmed Zayed — Zayed Art.

Enjoy smooth gameplay, colorful visuals, multiple game modes, rewarding challenges, and a wide range of customization options.

Whether you're chasing a new high score or enjoying a relaxing round, Za Snake brings a fresh experience to a timeless arcade favorite.

05 / OPTIONAL CONNECTIVITY

Google Play Games Integration

Play your way — online features are optional.

Za Snake includes optional Google Play Games integration intended to support player authentication, game achievements, and cloud-based progress features.

When available and enabled, Google Play Games sign-in may access your Google Play Games player identity to connect your gaming profile with supported game features.

Google Play Games connectivity and cloud synchronization are currently subject to service configuration, device compatibility, network availability, and age-related restrictions. Some features may not be available.

✓

A Google account is not required to enjoy the core game. Players can continue playing with locally saved progress without signing in.

06 / PRIVACY FIRST

Privacy & Player Safety

Za Snake is designed for a broad audience, including children aged 6 and older.

The game uses age-appropriate controls to manage access to optional online services. Where permitted, it may display advertisements through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded advertisements.

Gameplay data is stored locally by default. Certain Google services may process information when their features are available and used, as explained in our Privacy Policy.

We are committed to transparency about how information is used and to providing an enjoyable gaming experience.
